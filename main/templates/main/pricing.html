The prices shown above are WebOra's website design and development charges only.

  • Email services where applicable
  • Other external infrastructure costs
Please note: Domain, hosting, deployment, paid APIs, third-party services and other external costs are not included in the development fee and are paid separately by the client.

Final pricing may vary depending on project requirements, number of pages and advanced functionality.
